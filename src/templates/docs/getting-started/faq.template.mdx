## Does the frame emit light in the bedroom or at night?
**No, absolutely none.** 

Zenso uses reflective electronic paper with physical color pigment microcapsules. It produces zero backlight, zero blue light, and zero glare, looking just like museum-grade archival paper print under ambient room lighting. At night when the room lights are off, the frame is completely dark.

---

## What happens when the battery runs out?
**The image stays on the screen forever.**

The ACeP e-paper technology is bi-stable. This means the micro-pigments stay locked in position indefinitely with zero electrical power. The last displayed image or family photo remains crisp and visible forever until you plug in a standard USB-C charger.

---

## Which Wi-Fi networks are supported?
**Standard 2.4 GHz 802.11 b/g/n networks.**

Zenso connects to standard 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi (supported by virtually all home broadband routers). 5 GHz-only bands are not supported by the hardware radio. If your home Wi-Fi broadcasts separate SSID names for 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, connect to the 2.4 GHz network during initial onboarding.

---

## Can multiple family members send photos to the frame?
**Yes.**

You can share your frame's private invite link or pairing QR code with trusted family members through [panel.zenso.ink](https://panel.zenso.ink) so they can contribute photos to your shared album without needing technical knowledge.
